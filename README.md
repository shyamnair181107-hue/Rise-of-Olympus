# ALEGRIA — RISE OF OLYMPUS
> **"WHERE MORTALS BECOME LEGENDS."**
> Official Cultural Extravaganza Website | Mahatma Education Society's Pillai Group of Institutions

---

## 🏛️ Project Identity & Overview

**ALEGRIA – Rise of Olympus** is an interview-level frontend digital experience designed and built for one of India's biggest inter-collegiate cultural festivals. Inspired by ancient Greek mythology merged with modern cinematic web aesthetics, this website transports visitors straight into Mount Olympus.

### 🌟 Key Themes & Design System
- **Midnight Navy & Royal Blues:** `#080D1B`, `#0B1226`, `#101C39`
- **Imperial & Antique Gold:** `#D4AF62`, `#A77B32`, `#F3E5AB`
- **Celestial Lighting & Marble:** `#6BA7E8`, `#F4F0E7`, `#C8CBD3`
- **Typography:**
  - Majestic Titles: *Cinzel* & *Cinzel Decorative*
  - Classical Editorial Epigraphs: *Cormorant Garamond*
  - Modern Legibility: *Montserrat* & *Inter*
- **Visual Flourishes:**
  - Greek key / meander geometric borders
  - Ambient golden starfield & celestial particle canvas
  - Distant golden lightning flashes
  - Floating Greek marble colonnades & Zeus statuary
  - Glassmorphic panels with subtle gold-rim illumination

## ⚡ Sections & Experience Flow

1. **Initial Awakening Screen:**
   - Animated Greek emblem with *"AWAKENING OLYMPUS..."* and smooth transition into the experience.
2. **Floating Navbar:**
   - Glassmorphic navigation with active section indicator, smooth scroll routing, and mobile drawer menu.
3. **Cinematic Hero:**
   - Full-screen Olympus environment with floating marble architecture, parallax Zeus statuary, golden particle field, dual CTAs (*Explore the Festival*, *Discover the Events*), and scroll cue.
4. **The Mythology / Story:**
   - Editorial layout: *"BEYOND THE CLOUDS. BEYOND THE ORDINARY."*
   - Pillai campus festival statistics counter: 50,000+ Mortals, 100+ Contests, ₹15L+ Prize Bounty, 350+ Colleges.
5. **The Gods of Olympus (Pantheon of Power):**
   - 6 interactive character cards with 3D tilt, elemental auras, and interactive modal lore:
     1. **ZEUS** (King of the Gods • Lightning)
     2. **POSEIDON** (Lord of the Seas • Water)
     3. **HADES** (Lord of the Underworld • Darkness)
     4. **ATHENA** (Goddess of Wisdom • Wisdom & Strategy)
     5. **APOLLO** (God of Light • Sun & Music)
     6. **ARES** (God of War • Fire & Athletic Might)
6. **The Arena of Legends (Festival Events):**
   - 8 major festival categories: Performing Arts, Gaming Arena, Sports & Athletics, Fine Arts, Literary Arts, Management & Conclave, Technical & Innovation, Informals & Fun.
   - Interactive modal to inspect sub-events, prize pools, venues, rules, and instant pass enlisting.
7. **Experience Section:**
   - 4 flagship pillars: Live Performances, Unforgettable Experiences, Epic Competitions, Festival Spirit.
8. **Cinematic Aftermovie:**
   - *"RELIVE THE LEGEND"* 4K teaser container with interactive play modal.
9. **Visual Archives Gallery:**
   - Masonry layout with category filter tabs and full Lightbox with Next/Prev/Close controls.
10. **Voices of Alegria:**
    - Verified appreciation quotes from artists and dignitaries including Armaan Malik, Zakir Khan, Dr. K. M. Vasudevan Pillai, and Nucleya.
11. **Final Call to Action:**
    - *"YOUR LEGEND STARTS HERE."* with instant entry to Olympus.
12. **Divine Registration & Passes:**
    - Modal pass selector (Demigod Day Pass ₹299, Olympian All-Access ₹799, Titan VIP ₹1,999) with real-time pass generation and celebratory confetti.
13. **Audio Synthesis Player:**
    - Ambient Greek Dorian harp & celestial drone synthesized natively via Web Audio API (zero audio file dependencies, 100% reliable).
14. **Sophisticated Dark Footer:**
    - Greek meander border, contact details, Pillai campus venue information, social links, and Return-to-Apex button.
